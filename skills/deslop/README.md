# deslop

Strips AI writing patterns out of prose: filler openers, "not X, it's Y" contrasts, em dashes, three-item lists, "delve" and friends. Say "deslop this" or "make it sound human," or let it kick in whenever Claude drafts something that should read like a person wrote it.

Comes with a quick checklist and a 50-point score for reviewing text. Works for blog posts, emails, reports, and scientific writing.
